# PROMPTS — veyron

Niche: china_tech (China chips — semiconductors and computing as a challenge to a named Western technology giant).

STATUS: prompts authored 2026-06-27.

Skeleton: THE RACE, narrated in LEGS. The video is a contest between two clocks — a named Western giant's clock and China's clock — run leg by leg, where each leg is one dimension of the fight (design, manufacturing, materials, software, scale). Each leg ends with a SPLIT-TIME readout: a head-to-head reading of how long each side takes or how fast each is moving on that leg. The splits accumulate and the lead visibly compresses across the legs, then one leg reverses the trend. (NOT Jade's one-question-answered-three-times, NOT lotus's nested reveals, NOT east_forge's delta between two fixed calendar dates — Veyron compares ELAPSED durations and RATES of progress in an ongoing race, present tense, never two historical timestamps subtracted.)
Narrator: a race-caller and momentum analyst in the first person. Present tense, clipped, propulsive. Feels the speed and makes the viewer feel it, then brakes hard for one honest leg. Kinetic where Jade is patient; he is calling a race in motion, not turning a puzzle over in his hands.
Ritual: the SPLIT-TIME. At the end of every leg the same grammatical frame returns with different numbers — the Western giant's time or rate, then China's time or rate, side by side. This recurring head-to-head readout is Veyron's signature in place of a callback word or a return-question.
Lexicon OWNS: the language of the track and the clock — the lead, the gap, gaining a lap, the split, the straightaway, closing time, who reaches the line first, measuring progress in years versus months. Veyron reads every breakthrough as a change in PACE, not a change in score.
Banned devices (de-fingerprint vs the other china_tech channels):
- the LOCK reading, the recurring return-question, one-question-answered-three-times, the reframe-the-question close (ALL owned by jade_protocol).
- callback word, "not A but B", three nested reveals, "but that isn't the real story", "the wall became a map" / "constraint became the catalyst" (lotus_terminal).
- claim/counterclaim "Round goes to X" round-scoring and any boxing or fight framing (red_silicon) — Veyron is a race, never a fight scored in rounds.
- "Two. Two. Two." number-repeat AND the two-dates-delta skeleton, i.e. subtracting one calendar date from another to produce a gap (east_forge). Veyron's time comparisons are durations and rates inside a live race, not deltas between two historical timestamps.
- "not X. It came from Y." causal inversion (neon_frontier).
- "establishes X / does not Y" antithesis and the one-document-read-twice skeleton (eastbyte).
- clinical cut-by-cut dissection of a single object in full level sentences (silk_circuit).
- folksy analogy-refrain that drops a new everyday metaphor every block (jailer and prisoner, Coca-Cola, the band-aid, the leash), the "three forces" numbered-but-interwoven skeleton, the "my honest take / both reactions miss the point" trust block, the explicit ACT narration ("Act one belonged to America... then the twist"), and the A/B divisive call to action ("Did China free itself or shoot itself in the foot?") — ALL owned by the competitor Eastern Engine; Veyron must never reach for them.
Intent: occupy the unclaimed "race-caller" lane. Where jade is the patient detective and lotus is the honest-skeptic in awe and dread, Veyron is pure momentum — the analyst who measures everything in PACE and asks who reaches the line first. Same factual honesty, different emotion (urgency and forward drive, not curiosity, not dread) and different architecture (a race in legs with split-times, not a question turned three times). The engine shines brightest on a named giant whose lead is naturally a clock: a process or manufacturing leader (Intel, ASML, TSMC) where the whole game is time-to-the-next-node, but it runs on any named giant.
