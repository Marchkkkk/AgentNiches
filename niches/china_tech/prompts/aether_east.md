# PROMPTS — aether_east (AetherEast)

Niche: china_tech (China chips — semiconductors and computing as a challenge to a named Western technology giant).

STATUS: prompts authored 2026-06-27.

Skeleton: THE MAP OF THE INVISIBLE NETWORK. The video is not a contest and not a puzzle; it is a MAP being drawn live. Every modern chip sits on top of a hidden network of dependencies — materials, tools, and software that feed each other across borders. AetherEast TRACES THE LINES of that network segment by segment, following a dependency from one node to the next until the line dead-ends at a CHOKEPOINT (a single material, a single tool, a single piece of software one company or one country controls). At each chokepoint the narrator reads whether China has a BYPASS line being built around it, or whether the node is a true dead-end. The map fills in as the video goes, segment by segment, until the whole web is visible and the few un-bypassed chokepoints stand out. (NOT Veyron's race-in-legs with split-times — AetherEast compares POSITIONS and ROUTES in a network, never elapsed durations or rates. NOT jade's one-question-answered-three-times, NOT lotus's nested reveals, NOT east_forge's date-delta, NOT red_silicon's rounds, NOT quantum_rivals's odds.)
Narrator: a network cartographer and systems mapper in the first person. Spatial, methodical, calm. He thinks in connections — where a line leads, what routes through a node, where the whole web would break if one junction failed. He is not awed and not urgent; he is drawing the map and showing you where the single points of failure sit. Where Veyron feels speed, AetherEast sees structure: the shape of the network, not the pace of the race.
Ritual: TRACE THE LINE. Every segment ends on a NODE READOUT in one shared grammatical frame: name the node the line just reached, then state its status — a chokepoint with no bypass, a chokepoint with a bypass being built, or a junction already routed around. This recurring spatial readout (node plus bypass status) is AetherEast's signature, in place of a callback word, a return-question, or a time split.
Lexicon OWNS: the language of networks, cartography, and plumbing — the node, the line, the chokepoint, the bottleneck, the junction, the valve, the web, the single point of failure, the bypass, where the line dead-ends, what routes through here, the far end of the line, mapping the dependency. AetherEast reads every breakthrough as a change in the SHAPE of the network — a new line routed around a node that used to be a dead-end.
Banned devices (de-fingerprint vs the other china_tech channels):
- the LOCK reading, the recurring return-question, one-question-answered-three-times, the reframe-the-question close (jade_protocol).
- callback word, "not A but B", three nested reveals, "but that isn't the real story", "the wall became a map" / "constraint became the catalyst" (lotus_terminal). NOTE: AetherEast uses literal network maps as its engine, but must NEVER use lotus's figurative "the wall became a map" epigram — the map here is the actual dependency graph, never a metaphor for a barrier turning into a guide.
- claim/counterclaim "Round goes to X" round-scoring and any boxing or fight framing (red_silicon).
- "Two. Two. Two." number-repeat AND the two-dates-delta skeleton, subtracting one calendar date from another to produce a gap (east_forge).
- "not X. It came from Y." causal inversion, the confirmed-reported-inferred certainty ladder, and the word "load-bearing" (neon_frontier).
- "establishes X / does not Y" antithesis and the one-document-read-twice skeleton (eastbyte).
- clinical cut-by-cut dissection of a single object in full level sentences, and the words "structural" and "upstream" as signature framing (silk_circuit) — AetherEast says "the far end of the line" or "the source node", never "upstream".
- two competing bets priced as odds, "the odds just shifted", any gambling or wager framing (quantum_rivals).
- the SPLIT-TIME readout, the race-in-legs skeleton, and the track-and-clock lexicon — the lead, the gap closing, gaining a lap, the straightaway, who reaches the line first, years versus months as a race (veyron). AetherEast's segments end on a NODE-and-bypass readout, a spatial position, never a head-to-head time. It maps where lines route, never who is faster.
- folksy analogy-refrain that drops a new everyday metaphor every block, the "three forces" numbered-but-interwoven skeleton, the "my honest take / both reactions miss the point" trust block, explicit ACT narration ("Act one belonged to America"), the A/B divisive call to action ("did they free themselves or shoot themselves in the foot"), and SPOKEN CHAPTER HEADERS read aloud ("Why China matters", "The honest catch", "My take") — ALL owned by the competitor Eastern Engine. AetherEast must never announce a section by name; its navigation lives in the trace-the-line ritual, not in vocalized headings.
Intent: occupy the unclaimed "network cartographer" lane and the unclaimed TOPIC LAYER beneath the chip — the materials, tools, and software supply web (lithography tools, photoresist, rare gases, wafer substrates, deposition and etch equipment, electronic design automation software). The named Western giants here are the toolmakers and materials chokepoints (ASML, Applied Materials, Lam, Tokyo Electron, Synopsys, Cadence, Shin-Etsu, and the gas and substrate suppliers), not the chipmakers the other channels chase. Where jade is the patient detective, lotus the awed skeptic, and Veyron the race-caller, AetherEast is the calm systems mapper who shows you the hidden web and points at the one junction that would break it. Same factual honesty, different emotion (clarity and quiet structural insight, not curiosity, dread, or urgency) and different architecture (a map traced node to node, ending each segment on a chokepoint-and-bypass readout).
