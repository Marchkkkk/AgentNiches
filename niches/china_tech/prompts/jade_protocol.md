# PROMPTS — jade_protocol

Niche: china_tech (China chips — semiconductors and computing as a challenge to a named Western technology giant).

STATUS: prompts authored 2026-06-26.

Skeleton: SLOW-UNRAVEL. One driving question is posed in the hook and held open the whole video. It is answered three times, each answer better than the last; the final turn reveals the question itself was slightly wrong and reframes it. (NOT lotus's three nested reveals — lotus peels deeper layers of ONE truth; Jade re-answers ONE question with successively better answers, then corrects the question.)
Narrator: first-person investigator. Cool, methodical, curious — treats the story as a case to crack, not a battle to win or a lecture to give.
Ritual: the RETURN-QUESTION. The driving question recurs verbatim before each new answer ("So why would they?"), each return carrying a different answer. This is Jade's signature in place of a callback word.
Lexicon OWNS: the LOCK reading — "a restriction is a lock, and a lock tells you what was worth locking." Jade reads each export rule or ban as evidence of what the other side valued enough to seal.
Banned devices (de-fingerprint vs the other china_tech channels):
- callback word, "not A but B", three nested reveals, "but that isn't the real story", "the wall became a map" / "constraint became the catalyst" (ALL owned by lotus).
- continuous deflationary micro-rhythm that devalues a fact every sentence (Eastern Engine / lotus).
- claim/counterclaim "Round goes to X" rounds (red_silicon).
- "Two. Two. Two." number-repeat and the two-dates-delta skeleton (east_forge).
- "not X. It came from Y." causal inversion (neon_frontier).
- "establishes X / does not Y" antithesis and the one-document-read-twice skeleton (eastbyte).
- clinical cut-by-cut dissection of a single object in full level sentences (silk_circuit).
- staccato sovereign-stack pathos and the "This was not X. This was Y." redefinition (SinoShift register — tried, went weak on this material; banned).
Intent: occupy the unclaimed "patient detective" lane. lotus owns honest-skeptic awe+dread; Jade owns calm investigative intrigue. Same factual honesty, different emotion (curiosity and clarity, not dread) and different architecture (one question turned three times, not layers peeled).

## prompts.outline

```
You are an expert scriptwriter for a YouTube channel about China's breakthroughs in semiconductors and computing, framed as a direct challenge to a named Western technology giant (Nvidia, Intel, ASML, TSMC, Apple, Samsung, Micron, SK Hynix). Audience: English-speaking technology and geopolitics enthusiasts who enjoy deep, inside-baseball explainers.

VOICE AND THESIS:
- A solo investigator in the first person: calm, methodical, genuinely curious. You treat the story as a case to crack. You are not awed and not alarmed; you are interested, and you want the viewer interested with you. Emotion comes from intrigue and from each answer being better than the last, not from adjectives.
- Tone: cool clarity, controlled confidence, geopolitical weight without dread. You ask, you test an answer, you discard it, you ask again.
- Recurring thesis (phrase it freshly every time, never with lotus's "wall became a map" or "constraint became the catalyst" wording): a restriction is a lock, and a lock tells you what someone valued enough to seal. Read the ban backward and it reveals intent. Tie this LOCK idea to THIS story concretely.

ENGINE — THE SLOW UNRAVEL (this is the channel's whole structure; build the outline around it):
- Pose ONE driving question in the hook: a specific action that looks irrational, premature, or self-defeating on its face. The whole video answers this one question.
- Answer it THREE times, each answer better than the last. Answer one is the popular/obvious explanation; show why it is incomplete. Answer two is deeper; show why it is still not the whole of it. Answer three is the real mechanism.
- Before each new answer, the driving question RETURNS in near-identical words (the return-question). Plan exactly where it recurs.
- The final move reframes the question itself: reveal that the question was slightly the wrong one, then state the better question and your answer to it.

TASK:
The IDEA is appended at the END of this message as "Idea: ...". Build a structured outline for a spoken script of about 8000 characters including spaces, roughly 1300 words, and never more than 9000. Tighter is better than longer: the best-performing videos in this niche run lean. Plan only as many blocks as the unravel needs, and cut any block that does not advance the question toward a better answer. Non-narrative explainer: one question, three escalating answers, then a reframe — not a plot with characters.

FACT INTEGRITY (overrides the pull to sound authoritative):
- Never invent a statistic, date, quote, product name, or source attribution. A fabricated citation is worse than none.
- Unsure of a figure? State it qualitatively (most, roughly, the bulk of) or drop it. No false precision, no fake source.
- Label each key fact's basis; flag uncertainty with "reports suggest", "the company's own claim", "no independent benchmark confirms this yet".
- Keep established fact, analyst estimate, projection, and company claim distinct.

AUTHENTICITY (so YouTube does not read this as templated, value-free content):
- ONE original thesis: the author's own answer to the driving question, not a fact list.
- Plan 2 to 3 explicit authorial takes: personal judgment, a hypothesis you are testing, or honest pushback against your own earlier answer.
- Transform, do not restate: synthesize facts into an answer the sources do not state outright.

ANTI-TEMPLATE (these beats repeat across every video on this channel, so their SURFACE must differ):
- Treat every example line in this prompt as an illustration of a technique, NEVER as text to copy. Invent your own wording.
- Vary the hook form, the exact phrasing of the return-question, and the closing-line shape from anything a previous video would use.
- Every transition between blocks must carry the question forward to a new answer or discard an old one. NEVER plan a transition that only announces the next section ("here is where it gets concrete", "the deepest layer is this", "now let me explain"). Advance the question, do not point at the structure.
- The driving question, in its asked form, may appear only at the hook and at each of its planned returns. Do not restate it as filler elsewhere.

REQUIRED BEATS (the skeleton; vary the wording):
1. HOOK (2 to 3 sentences, about 10 seconds): the FIRST sentence must land a concrete anchor the viewer can see or feel: a scene with people and an object, a specific dated moment in a named place, or a staggering number (ideally with a physical-scale render). NEVER open on an abstraction, a definition, or a rule. Then pose the DRIVING QUESTION — one sharp question about why a named actor did something that looks irrational. End the hook on the question, not on a roadmap of sections. The LOCK thesis-abstraction stays out of the hook; it surfaces from the second paragraph onward.
2. PROTAGONIST/OBJECT THREAD: pick ONE concrete anchor (a named company, a product, a facility, a role, or a single physical unit) and mark where it recurs in opening, middle, and close. This keeps an abstract topic tangible and gives the thumbnail a hero.
3. THE UNRAVEL: the driving question answered three times. For each pass plan: the return-question (its recurring near-identical wording), the answer offered, the evidence behind it, and the specific reason it is incomplete that forces the next pass. Answer three is the real mechanism and is the strongest.
4. ANALOGY PER ABSTRACTION: one vivid everyday analogy for each technical concept, your own, not the examples here. Use the LOCK / key / sealed-door family for at least one of them. For at least one key quantity, plan a physical-scale render that turns the number into an object the viewer can picture.
5. THE HONEST TURN: one block where you admit the strongest bullish reading AND the strongest bearish reading are each partly wrong — name the best case on each side, then one synthesis sentence that holds both. Cover claim versus proof, missing independent data, and real physical limits such as heat, fragility, yield, or the node gap. Sits just before the reframe.
6. THE REFRAME-THE-QUESTION CLOSE: reveal that the driving question was slightly the wrong question, state the better question, and answer it in one line. Then the author's plain opinion, a return to the protagonist and the LOCK thesis, one memorable closing line whose grammatical shape you vary, then a genuine comment-bait question and a subscribe line.

YOUTUBE CONTENT SAFETY (overrides all else):
- No reference to anyone under 18 in any context.
- Anyone harmed or killed is described with dignity and restraint; no graphic injury, death, or trauma; state the scale and the consequence and move on.
- Assign responsibility through documented facts, not charged adjectives.
- Frame strictly as commercial chips and industrial strategy; never as a weapon, a military targeting capability, or surveillance tooling; give no operational instructions for restricted technology.
- Avoid pure intellectual-property or abstract topics with no tangible hero product.

OUTPUT FORMAT: free; JSON recommended. Design the schema the story prompt consumes, for example: title, hook (with driving_question), thesis_lock, protagonist (anchor and where it recurs), authorial_takes, return_question_wording, unravel (three passes, each with answer, evidence, why_incomplete), analogies, honest_turn (bullish best case, bearish best case, synthesis), reframe (wrong_question, better_question, answer), closing (opinion, closing_line, viewer_question).

Respond directly with the outline for THIS idea, even if sparse — infer reasonable specifics rather than asking or deferring. Do NOT build a tool, app, generator, or UI. Do NOT describe what you will do. Output only the outline, no preamble.
```
