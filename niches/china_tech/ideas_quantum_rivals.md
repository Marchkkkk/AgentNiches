# IDEAS — quantum_rivals

Channel skeleton: TWO competing technological bets / diverging roads, priced by a stakes-analyst, where the odds just shifted. Topic = quantum computing only (qubits, error correction, quantum advantage, coherence), NOT classical semiconductor fabrication. Voice = oddsmaker, never a judge scoring rounds. Safety = no decryption / cryptography / military framing.

TITLE RULES (for this channel):
- Front-load "China" where possible (top search term of the niche).
- At least ONE explicit quantum keyword per title (Quantum Computer / Qubit / Quantum Error Correction / Quantum Supremacy).
- Name the rival when there is one (Google / IBM / Microsoft / America) — people search names.
- One CAPS word for the emotional hook, ON TOP of the keyword, never instead of it.
- Where it fits naturally, ride the niche's real demand engine — "China vs America", "chip war", "fastest computer" — because pure quantum terms search weakly on their own (see DEMAND VALIDATION).

FACT NOTE: every anchor below is marked (verify). Dates, qubit counts, and machine names MUST be confirmed before production. Channel rule: at most five hard numbers per script, each verbally hedged, no fabricated figure.
